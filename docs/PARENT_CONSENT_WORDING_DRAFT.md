## OVERVIEW

This document provides suggested consent language for parents/guardians regarding:
- Use of Lauris Learn platform
- Data collection and privacy
- Document sharing with educational partners
- Communication preferences
- Optional features and services

Schools should have legal counsel review and customize these templates for their jurisdiction and specific use cases.

---

## 1. BASIC PLATFORM CONSENT

### Simple Version (for younger families or lower reading levels)

**What is Lauris Learn?**  
Lauris Learn is an app that helps our school and families stay connected. Teachers use it to:
- Record attendance
- Share class updates and photos
- Report on your child's progress
- Share important documents

As a parent, you will:
- See your child's attendance and progress
- Receive class announcements
- Respond to school events (like RSVPs)
- Communicate securely with teachers

**What information do we collect?**  
We collect:
- Your child's name, date of birth, contact info
- Attendance, grades, and progress reports
- Medical/allergy information for safety
- Photos of your child (if you consent separately)
- Your contact information (email, phone)

**Is my information secure?**  
Yes. We use:
- Passwords to protect accounts
- Encryption to protect data in transit and storage
- Backups in case of emergency
- Limited access — only staff and family can see your information

**Can teachers share my child's information with others?**  
Not without permission. If therapy, medical, or other service providers need access, the school will ask your consent first.

**Do you use my information for marketing?**  
No. We do not use student information for marketing, advertising, or selling.

**Consent checkbox:**
- ☐ I agree to my child's use of Lauris Learn and this privacy practice.

### Full Version (comprehensive legal language)

**PARENTAL CONSENT FOR LAURIS LEARN PLATFORM USE**

**Purpose:** The school uses Lauris Learn, an online platform, to manage student records, communicate with families, and facilitate educational coordination.

**Data Collection:** By enrolling your child, you consent to collection and processing of:

1. **Identification Information:**
   - Legal name, preferred name
   - Date of birth
   - Gender identity (if provided)
   - Student ID number and Learning Registration Number (LRN)
   - Profile photo (optional)

2. **Educational Records:**
   - Class enrollment and schedule
   - Attendance records (dates, presence/absence/tardy/excused)
   - Grade and progress observations
   - Teacher notes and evaluations
   - Behavior reports
   - Disciplinary records (if applicable)
   - IEPs, 504 plans, or special accommodations (if applicable)

3. **Health & Safety Information:**
   - Allergies and medical conditions
   - Medications (if applicable)
   - Emergency contact information
   - Authorized pickup contacts
   - Special needs or accessibility requirements

4. **Family Information:**
   - Parent/guardian names and contact information
   - Relationship to student
   - Household information (if needed for school meals/services)

5. **Access & Usage Data:**
   - Login times and frequency
   - Documents viewed or downloaded
   - IP address and device information (for security)
   - Dates and times of access

**Data Protection:**
- All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- Access is restricted by role (teachers see only their students; parents see only their child)
- All access is logged for security and audit purposes
- Unauthorized access triggers alerts and investigation
- Data is backed up daily and encrypted in backup storage

**Data Usage — Permitted:**
- Display to authorized school staff (administrators, teachers, support staff)
- Display to parents/guardians and the student (if age-appropriate)
- School operations (attendance reporting, grade tracking, communication)
- Legal compliance (state reporting, audit, investigations)
- Safety and welfare (emergency contact, medical information)

**Data Usage — Not Permitted:**
- Marketing or advertising
- Sale to third parties
- Use for teacher/student profiling
- Use outside of school operations
- Sharing with other schools or districts without consent

**External Sharing:**
- The school may share documents with educational partners (therapists, medical providers, evaluation centers) with explicit written consent
- External sharing requires documented consent for each instance
- External parties can only access documents specifically shared with them
- External access is time-limited and can be revoked at any time
- All external access is logged for audit purposes

**Your Rights:**
- **View:** You can view all information about your child within the app
- **Update:** You can correct demographic information (with school approval)
- **Access:** You can request a complete copy of all records we hold about your child
- **Restrict:** You can restrict sharing with external parties
- **Revoke:** You can revoke external sharing at any time
- **Delete:** You can request deletion of information (educational records exempted per law)

**Withdrawal:**
- You may withdraw from Lauris Learn by requesting to use an alternative communication method
- However, the school will maintain attendance, grades, and other required educational records
- Billing and payment history will be retained per tax law requirements

**Questions?**  
If you have questions about:
- How your data is used: [contact administrator]
- Privacy concerns: [contact privacy officer]
- Security issues: [contact IT support]
- External sharing decisions: [contact school office]

**Consent:**

I have read and understand this consent. I agree to the collection and use of information about my child as described above.

Parent/Guardian Name (print): _____________________  
Parent/Guardian Signature: _____________________  
Date: _____________________  
Student Name: _____________________  
Student ID: _____________________  

**□ Consent to all practices above**  
**□ Consent with restrictions (specify below):**
- _______________________________________________

## 2. DOCUMENT SHARING CONSENT

### When Parents Must Consent (Examples)

**Before Sharing IEP/Special Ed Records:**

"Your child receives special education services. To coordinate services with [Therapist/Medical Provider], the school needs to share your child's IEP and medical records with them. This sharing will allow them to provide consistent, coordinated care.

**What we'll share:**
- IEP document and progress reports
- Medical/allergy information
- Teacher observations relevant to the therapist's role

**Who we'll share with:**
- [Name of therapist/clinic]
- [Address/location]

**Duration:**
- Valid from [date] to [date] (typically one academic year)
- You can revoke at any time

**External party's obligations:**
- Keep information confidential
- Use it only for [child's] treatment/education
- Return or destroy records when done

**Do you consent?**
- ☐ Yes, I consent to share these records
- ☐ No, do not share with [provider name]"

---

"To ensure [child's] safety and health at school, the school nurse may share medical information with:
- Teachers who work directly with your child
- Athletic coaches (if applicable)
- Counselors or support staff

**What we'll share:**
- Allergies and medication needs
- Medical conditions relevant to school activities
- Emergency medical information

**Consent:**
- ☐ Yes, share relevant medical information with school staff
- ☐ No, keep medical information confidential (emergency-only)"

**Before Sharing with Outside Evaluators:**

**Evaluator:** [Name], License #[number]

- ☐ Yes, share records and schedule evaluation

## 10. IMPLEMENTATION GUIDANCE

### For School Staff

1. **Obtain consent early:**
   - Provide during enrollment
   - Use translated versions as needed
   - Explain in family conference if requested

2. **Document consent:**
   - Keep signed copies on file
   - Scan and store securely
   - Flag students with restrictions/refusals

3. **Honor preferences:**
   - Respect "no photos" requests
   - Use alternative communication for opt-outs
   - Regularly review and refresh consent

4. **Update for changes:**
   - New features require new consent
   - Major data uses require new consent
   - Annual refresh is best practice

5. **Handle withdrawals:**
   - Parents can withdraw at any time
   - Use alternative method going forward
   - Retain historical records per law
